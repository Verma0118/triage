# DEMO VIDEO: TRIAGE — AI air defense for small outposts

**Length:** 60–65 seconds. **Tone:** calm, realistic, tense but not dramatic.
No explosions, no gore, no real unit insignia. An intercept is a clean disappearance.

**Structure:** 5 seconds of setup, then one continuous screen recording of the real software,
composited onto the tablet with 3D cutaways over it.

---

## How the tablet footage is made

1. Open Triage, press **RUN FULL DEMO**, screen-record until it stops. **One take, ~58 seconds,
   hands-free.** It runs night 1, the capability gap, the after-action report, the module swap
   and night 2, approving each decision on a beat so the **APPROVED ✓** flash reads on camera.
2. Composite that recording onto the tablet in the 3D scene.
3. **Sync reference:** the app prints its own clock in the header (`SIM 11.7 s`). Marks below are
   **D+**, seconds after the button is pressed. The on-screen clock is ground truth.

**Capture:** full-screen the browser so no tabs or address bar show, hide the cursor, and don't
touch the machine during the take — any click stops the runner.

## Notes for the editor

- **One unbroken take**, ~58 seconds. Every string above is what the software actually prints.
- **Timings are measured**, but the `SIM` clock in the header is the real timecode — use it to
  line the 3D cutaways to frames.
- **Unit 1 is a real drone.** Its voltage, percentage and attitude horizon are live telemetry
  over USB from an actual flight controller. Worth one insert of the physical drone beside the
  tablet, tilting it so the horizon on screen follows.
- **The GLB, the physical drone and the Variant B BOM are the same airframe.** Launch shots,
  exploded view and the hardware on the table should read as one object. Match the 3D lighting to
  the night palette of the UI, not a studio look.
- **Variant A and Variant B must be visually distinguishable** — different motors and props, same
  core — or night 2 doesn't land.
- **If it needs to be tighter**, trim the quiet stretch D+14→D+20 and the after-action hold
  D+40→D+44. Do not compress the recall, the impact, or the Velocity card.
- **Colour meanings follow the UI:** amber unknown/pending, red threat/alert, grey-taupe decoy.
  There are no friendly tracks in this demo — that label was removed to keep the film readable.
